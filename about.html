Our Legacy & Vision

About JAFLY Sports Complex

Bringing international-grade football infrastructure, healthy youth recreation, and hospitality to Jaflong, Sylhet.

Our Story

Born From Sylhet's Passion For Football

For decades, footballers in Gowainghat and greater Jaflong had to deal with waterlogged mud pitches during Sylhet's six-month monsoon season or travel 55 kilometers into Sylhet city just to play under floodlights.

Founded by passionate sports entrepreneurs from Sylhet, JAFLY Sports Complex transformed a scenic plot near Jaflong Zero Point into an all-weather FIFA-grade football arena, complete with the signature JAFLY Dugout Café and a family-friendly Kids Play Zone. Today, we serve both local athletes and thousands of tourists visiting Jaflong.

Our Mission

Empowering Youth & Community Fitness

To provide a safe, world-class, all-weather sporting environment that nurtures grassroots football talent in rural and suburban Sylhet while promoting active, screen-free lifestyles for youth and families.

Our Vision

Bangladesh's Most Scenic Sports Hub

To establish Jaflong not only as a natural tourist wonder, but as Bangladesh's premier sports-tourism destination hosting national 7-a-side tournaments, youth academies, and family leisure experiences.
