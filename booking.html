ℹ️ How This Booking System Works & Production Upgrade Path

Current Demo Mode (Client-Side Persistence): This page runs a fully interactive booking engine powered by Vanilla JavaScript and browser localStorage. When you book a slot, it validates your Bangladeshi phone number, calculates the exact Taka (৳) rate based on daytime (৳1,000) vs. evening floodlights (৳1,200), generates a booking reference, and permanently locks that slot on your browser for the chosen date so it cannot be double-booked.
